METHODOLOGY

What counts?

We track publicly reported events where AI materially participated in hacking, exploitation, cyber operations, or meaningful security research. Hypothetical claims and stories that merely mention AI do not count. See the full methodology for definitions, source rules, corrections, and edge cases.
